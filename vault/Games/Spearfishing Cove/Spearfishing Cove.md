# Spearfishing Cove
Low-poly Roblox spearfishing game. Dive into a stepped ocean pit, spear sea creatures, and bank the catch at the surface before the zone's guardian catches you. Full details: `README.md` in the repo root.

**Core loop:** spear a creature, carry it, escape the awake guardian (dash to dodge its lunge), surface to bank it, sell, upgrade.
**Zones:** Sunlit Shallows, Coral Reef, Twilight Trench, The Abyss, Leviathan's Rift (each with a guardian and fog colour).
**Systems:** chase and guardians, bounties, escape streak, sea index, shop and upgrades, bases with aquarium, Robux shop (IDs pending in `Config.lua`).
**Code:** `src/shared` (data and models), `src/server/Services`, `src/client/Controllers`.
**Tuning files:** `Config`, `Zones`, `FishData`, `GunData` in `src/shared`.
