# 5 After Launch
Source: one solo developer's TikTok advice (Oct 2026). Opinions, not facts. See [[Postmortem]].

25. **Keep updating a successful game.** Players stop spending when updates stop. Plan a schedule before launch.
26. **Set the maximum you can afford to lose** on a game, and don't go over it.
27. **Income is taxable at any age.** Set part of every payout aside before spending. Rules vary by country; ask a parent or trusted adult if you're young.
