# 3 Testing and Release
Source: one solo developer's TikTok advice (Oct 2026). Opinions, not facts. See [[Launch Checklist]].

12. **Test in rounds.** Friends first (4 to 5), then about 16 players through Discord, with an exclusive reward for testers. Plan 3 rounds.
13. **Write your launch bar down.** For example: no bugs that lose progress, core loop fun, onboarding clear, sound done. (He argues the first release should feel finished because few players makes people skip a game. Others say ship earlier. You decide.)
14. **Onboarding is the first five minutes.** Simple, short, hands-on. Players should be doing the core action within the first minute or two.
15. **Set up funnels before launch.** Define 5 to 8 steps (join, loading finished, tutorial, first round, second round...) and log them. Review them every few days.
16. **Read the numbers in pairs.** Retention, playtime, active users, funnel drop-offs. Change one thing at a time. A bad number plus a good one points at the problem.
17. **After a weak launch, fix the biggest funnel drop before deciding to quit.**
18. **Check the current "highly engaged player" requirement and review fees before choosing a launch date.** He said 250 now, dropping to 100 in November; verify on Roblox's own pages.
